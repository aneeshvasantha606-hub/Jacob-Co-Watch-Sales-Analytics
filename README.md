# Jacob & Co. Watch Sales Analytics

A luxury-watch retail analytics portfolio project built around a **synthetic dataset of 1,000 sales transactions** inspired by five Jacob & Co. watch model families. The workbook is intended for practicing data preparation, exploratory analysis, KPI development, and dashboard design in Excel, SQL, Python, and Power BI.

> **Data transparency:** All transaction-level sales, customer, discount, delivery, cost, and profit records in this workbook are synthetic examples. They are not actual Jacob & Co. customer records or company-reported sales. Prices are illustrative and model specifications may vary by reference and edition. This is an independent portfolio project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Jacob & Co.

## Project files

- `Dataset/Jacob_and_Co_Top_5_Watches_Sales_Dataset_1000_Rows.xlsx` — Excel workbook with 1,000 synthetic transaction rows, a model reference sheet, and a Read Me sheet.
- `Images/Dashboard_Background.png` — luxury-themed canvas background concept for a Power BI report.
- `PowerBI/` — reserved for the `.pbix` report and dashboard screenshots when available.

## Watch models covered

1. Bugatti Chiron Tourbillon
2. Opera Godfather
3. Astronomia Sky
4. Casino Tourbillon
5. Epic X Chrono

This is a curated five-model selection for a portfolio exercise, **not a verified best-selling ranking**. Public transaction-level unit sales are not available in the dataset.

## Dataset overview

The `Sales_Data` worksheet includes fields such as:

- Transaction ID and sale date
- Customer name, age, gender, city, and country (synthetic)
- Watch model, collection, reference label, and condition
- Sales channel, quantity, unit price, discount, and sales value
- Estimated cost and estimated profit
- Payment mode, delivery status, customer rating, and sales advisor
- Watch characteristics and product-page URLs
- A `Data_Type` label identifying the records as synthetic

The `Model_Reference` worksheet provides model-level reference information. `Read_Me` describes data assumptions and limitations.

## Suggested analysis questions

- Which model generates the highest simulated revenue and estimated profit?
- How do sales and average selling price vary by month?
- Which countries and cities have the highest simulated sales?
- How do new and pre-owned transactions compare?
- What is the effect of discounts on simulated revenue and margin?
- How do delivery status and customer ratings vary by sales channel?

## Suggested Power BI KPIs

- Total Simulated Sales
- Estimated Profit
- Estimated Profit Margin
- Number of Transactions
- Units Sold
- Average Selling Price
- Discount Rate
- Sales by Model, Country, and Month

**Reminder:** Label all report outputs as simulated/synthetic. Estimated cost and profit are generated for analytical practice and must not be represented as actual company financials.

1. Download the Excel workbook from `Dataset/`.
2. Import `Sales_Data` into Power BI or your preferred analysis tool.

5. Keep the synthetic-data disclaimer visible in any published portfolio output.
